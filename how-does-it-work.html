How Does Sheger Minibus Ride Work?

Sheger Minibus Ride helps newcomers and residents find information about minibus pickup locations and destinations from 4 Kilo.

Follow These Simple Steps

Step 1: Visit the homepage

Start by visiting our homepage to learn about Sheger Minibus Ride.

Step 2: Choose your destination

Check the available destinations from 4 Kilo on the Pickup Places page.

Step 3: Find Your Pickup Location

Read the pickup location information and check the map to identify where you should board your minibus.

Step 4: Confirm Before Boarding

Ask the minibus assistant or driver to confirm the destination and fare before boarding.

Important Travel Tips

  • Always confirm the destination before boarding.
  • fares and routes may change espically fares during nght time there might be changes
